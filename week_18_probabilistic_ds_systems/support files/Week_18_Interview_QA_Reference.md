#### Q1. When does this pattern provide superior performance over simpler linear scans?
*Verbal Answer:* When input bounds exceed 10^5 and repeated queries require sub-linear response time.

#### Q2. What are the key space vs time trade-offs?
*Verbal Answer:* Trading O(N) auxiliary space allows reducing query latencies from O(N) to O(log N) or O(1).
